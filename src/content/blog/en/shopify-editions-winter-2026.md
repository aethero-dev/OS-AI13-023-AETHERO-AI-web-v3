## The RenAIssance Edition

Shopify's Winter '26 Edition, dubbed **"The Renaissance Edition"**, introduces over 150 updates that merge artificial intelligence with e-commerce capabilities. The platform emphasizes practical AI tools, agentic sales, expanded B2B functions, and deeper integrations.

## Sidekick: Proactive AI assistant

### Sidekick Pulse

The standout feature of this edition. Sidekick PULSE moves beyond responding to commands to **anticipating needs**. The AI analyzes your shop data, economic trends, and e-commerce insights to propose concrete improvements — including automation suggestions, discounts, and email copy.

### Tinker App

A new application allows users to experiment with cutting-edge AI tools for online retail management before full deployment.

## Agentic Commerce

Products can now be sold directly through AI conversation platforms including ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity — creating sales channels beyond traditional websites.

## B2B & Catalog Enhancements

- Support for up to 2,048 product variants
- Streamlined business account creation
- Company-level credit management
- Enhanced pricing and collection controls

## Testing & Optimization

Integrated tools enable A/B testing and customer behavior simulation directly within the admin panel.
