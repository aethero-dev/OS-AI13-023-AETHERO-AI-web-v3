## Why platform selection matters

The choice of e-shop system is one of the most important decisions at the start, affecting project launch speed, future growth, expansion possibilities, and overall costs.

## Basic comparison

**Shopify** operates globally with 4.8 million shops and $292 billion in annual sales, offering 10,000+ apps across 170+ countries.

**Shoptet** serves 41,000 shops primarily in Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Hungary with approximately $4 billion USD in regional sales.

## Shoptet strengths

- Complete Czech language support (phone, chat, email)
- Native integration with Czech payment gateways (GoPay, ComGate, CSOB)
- Cash-on-delivery as standard
- Direct accounting system integration (Pohoda, Money S3, Abra)
- Free tier for up to 10 products
- Optimized for Czech tax legislation

**Shoptet is ideal for:** Local-only businesses targeting Czech/Slovak markets without international expansion plans.

## Shopify strengths

- Multi-language (20+) and multi-currency (130+) support
- 10,000+ global extensions with robust ERP/CRM integrations
- Advanced SEO, marketing automation, and analytics tools
- 24/7 support with extensive documentation
- Unlimited product capacity
- Supports omnichannel selling and headless commerce

**Shopify is ideal for:** International expansion, competitive markets, complex product catalogs, and businesses requiring advanced marketing capabilities.

## Key questions before choosing

1. Where will your customers be located?
2. Which payment and shipping methods matter?
3. How many products will you sell?
4. Do you need international expansion?
5. What's your budget for development and support?
6. How important is local language support?
7. Will you need ERP/CRM integrations?

## Model examples

**Local food shop:** Shoptet (rapid Czech setup, cash-on-delivery as standard)

**Competitive cycling shop in CZ:** Shopify (advanced SEO and marketing tools)

**Sportswear B2B/B2C exporter:** Shopify (global language and currency support)

**Digital courses, global audience:** Shopify (automation, international payments)

## Final recommendation

Choose **Shoptet** for purely local Czech/Slovak operations prioritizing quick, affordable launches.

Choose **Shopify** for international ambitions or competitive domestic markets requiring advanced marketing capabilities.
